## 1. Aspiring Entrepreneur Journey

### Profile: Sarah - Student with Disability
**Background:** Computer science student with visual impairment, has innovative EdTech idea
**Goals:** Find technical co-founder, connect with first investor, validate idea

### Journey Stages:

#### Stage 1: Discovery & Onboarding (Days 1-3)
- **Touchpoint:** Social media ad → Landing page
- **Actions:**
  - Uses screen reader to navigate homepage
  - Watches captioned demo video explaining platform value
  - Creates account with accessibility preferences
  - Completes progressive profile (basic info first)
- **Emotions:** Hopeful but cautious about accessibility
- **Pain Points:** Concerns about platform usability
- **Kolabolab Solutions:**
  - WCAG 2.2 AA compliant interface
  - Voice input for bio sections
  - High contrast mode available
  - Progressive profiling reduces cognitive load

#### Stage 2: Idea Validation (Days 4-14)
- **Actions:**
  - Posts EdTech idea with privacy controls
  - Receives AI-generated feedback summary
  - Connects with 3 relevant mentors from platform
  - Joins EdTech community within platform
- **Emotions:** Excited by positive feedback, building confidence
- **Key Features Used:**
  - Anonymous idea posting option
  - AI bias-checked feedback system
  - Community matching algorithm
  - Mentorship request system

#### Stage 3: Team Formation (Days 15-45)
- **Actions:**
  - Browses technical co-founder profiles
  - Uses accessibility-friendly matching filters
  - Initiates conversations with 5 candidates
  - Schedules video calls with live captions
  - Forms team with 2 co-founders
- **Emotions:** Nervous about team dynamics, excited about progress
- **Kolabolab Features:**
  - Anonymized initial matching (reduces bias)
  - Real-time collaboration tools
  - Equity calculator with screen reader support
  - Team formation templates

#### Stage 4: First Investor Connection (Days 46-90)
- **Actions:**
  - Prepares pitch using platform templates
  - Platform suggests 10 relevant angel investors
  - Submits applications to 5 investors
  - Receives meeting request from 2 investors
  - Successfully pitches to first investor
- **Emotions:** Anxious but prepared, thrilled by investor interest
- **Success Metrics:**
  - 2 investor meetings scheduled within 30 days
  - 40% higher success rate due to accessibility features
  - Team formed 60% faster than industry average
